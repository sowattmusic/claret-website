My Story

From Switzerland to London

In August 2025, I took my songs to Joe Lyons’ Palace Garden Studio in London.

I’d written them only a few days before. Then a drummer, a bassist, a guitarist and a keyboard player started playing them, and I heard things in those songs I hadn’t heard yet.

I was born in Sion, grew up in Lutry and started making music at ten. I grew up on the Beatles, James Brown and David Bowie.

I started out at Swiss Artists Productions, arranging and writing for other artists. In 2025, I co-wrote, composed and produced “Ce Soir”, the official anthem of Le Tremplin.

Today I’m a producer and artistic director at Sowatt Music. I like working out who should sing the first line, what happens when you take the guitar out, and which photo makes you want to hear the record.

Services

Studio · Live

  • Record production

    I produce singles, EPs and albums for solo artists, bands and labels. I can come in at the demo stage or take a project through pre-production, recording and delivery.

  • Songwriting and composition

    I co-write songs, melodies and lyrics with artists.

  • Original music for picture

    I compose scores and soundtracks for film, television, advertising and online video. I work with filmmakers, production companies, agencies and independent creators, writing everything from ambient underscore to experimental pieces built around a particular scene.

  • Arrangement

    I shape song structure, harmony, instrumentation and vocal parts for recordings and live performances.

  • Artistic direction

    I help artists define the sound of a project, choose the songs and connect the music to the artwork, photography and video.

  • Recording and vocal production

    I run sessions, coach performances and work on vocal arrangements, harmonies and takes.

  • Session planning

    I bring together musicians, studios and engineers for projects in Switzerland and London.

  • Live recording and live albums

    I record concerts and produce live albums, from the recording on the night to the finished release.

Through Sowatt Music

One team, from the first idea to the master

I’m an associate at Sowatt Music, a studio and production company in Crissier. I lead the artistic side: production, composition, arrangement and artistic direction. My partner Michel Gallone leads the technical side: recording, mixing and mastering. Together we can take a project all the way, under one roof. Beyond records, Sowatt also records live concerts and handles audiovisual work, including video transcription and multilingual subtitling for cultural institutions, educational projects and production companies.

Behind the recordTDenon · Album · 2026
TDenon on “Siegfried” and the orchestral ending · 12:01–12:58 ↗

Colosse aux Pieds d’Argiles

TDenon wanted an epic track. The result was “Siegfried,” a piece in three parts, each with its own musical identity. He says he still can’t fit it into a single genre.

For the album closer, we were talking about adding a couple of touches to the ending. As TDenon tells it, I put on my headphones, took them off about fifteen minutes later and said:

“I’m gonna make an orchestra.”

Those couple of touches turned into a symphonic ending.
